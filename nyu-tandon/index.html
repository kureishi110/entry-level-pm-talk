What I do in an entry‑level interview

Move one

“Tell me about yourself.”
Then I go quiet.

What it actually sounds like

Lands

“I'm the person who ends up asking whether we should build this at all. That started when we shipped something nobody used. It's why I want to talk about your onboarding problem.”

Loses me

“So, I grew up in New Jersey, did my undergrad in CS, interned at two places, the first was mostly data work, then I moved to the platform team, and after that I…”

Move two

“Walk me through this line on your resume.”

  • How did you grow it?
  • Was that what you expected?
  • What got in the way?
  • What surprised you?

“I increased subscription conversion by 7%”

  1. Over what time frame?
  2. What changed over that year?
  3. What else happened in that window, and how do you know it was you?

No A/B test. A team metric off a slide, claimed as their own.

“How are you using AI in your personal life?”

“I use AI for everything.”

Eye roll.

So I walk this ladder instead

  1. What problem were you having?
  2. Why did you reach for AI?
  3. What did you actually build?
  4. What was your first version?
  5. How did you know it was any good?
  6. What would you fix with infinite time?
